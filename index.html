An AI DevOps assistant

An interactive DevOps assistant built for NVHPC to automate routine operational workflows. I actively use it through Slack to request system reboots and inspect Slurm and job pools.

The need: offload routine operational work while maintaining round-the-clock awareness. The design aims to discover relevant context as it becomes familiar with the environment, answer node-health questions, and generate dynamic, contextual reports instead of static ones.

In development: automated failure diagnosis and diagnostic job reruns.
